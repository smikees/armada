ARMADA help

ARMADA runs a standing team of AI agents on your own computer, through your Claude, Codex, or Gemini login. Your team lives in a realm — a folder you own — and ARMADA is the app that runs it. Any one supported engine is enough, and you can connect more than one.

New here? Read Getting started, then How ARMADA thinks. Everything else is one page per part of the app — open the one you're looking at.
