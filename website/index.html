A team with context

Each agent has a role and its own memory. Conversations happen in threads, with shared context and the capabilities you choose to grant.

Work you can follow

Schedule recurring jobs, review their output and run history, and see specific failures or unresolved findings. ARMADA runs while open or in the tray.

A folder you own

Your agents, memories and artifacts live in a local realm folder. Back it up or move it. Model requests go to your selected provider.

Getting started

Install ARMADA and follow Alexander through setup. He helps check your CLI connections, choose a folder and appoint your first agents. ARMADA runs independently of the provider desktop apps.

Upgrading from an earlier beta? Run this installer once to update the bundled runtime. Your realms and settings are preserved.
