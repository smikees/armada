# Overview

The realm's dashboard. Across the top: how many agents, active jobs, runs in the last 30 days,
tokens used, and what those tokens would have cost on the API (you pay your subscription, not
this — it's there to compare).

## The widgets

- **Register** — the team. Each row: the agent, their model and effort, how many goals and jobs
  they own, their next run, their tokens, and a seven-square **week strip** (the last three days,
  today, the next three) coloured by how their jobs did. Click a row to open the agent.
- **Usage** — who (or which model) used how much, over a window you choose (today, 7 days, …).
- **Job calendar** — every scheduled run by day or week, coloured by outcome. Click a run to open
  its job.
- **Thread widgets** — pin any agent's thread to the dashboard to talk to them from here.

## Things you'll do

- **Rearrange:** drag a widget by its grip (the dotted handle at the top left); drag its right edge
  to resize.
- **Add or remove widgets:** **Manage widgets** lists what's on the dashboard and what you can add,
  including any agent's thread.
- **Give a widget its own page:** a widget's ⋮ menu → **Add as section** puts it in the top menu.
- **Appoint someone new:** **+ Appoint** on the Register.

## What the colours mean

The week strip and the calendar share one legend: **Success** (green), **Running** (teal),
**Scheduled** (outlined teal), **Warning** (amber), **Failed** (red), **Missed** (✕ — it should have
run and didn't), **Not scheduled** (grey).
